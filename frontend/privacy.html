Privacy policy

Last updated: March 2026

API Key Lab is a free, no-account utility that checks whether an AI provider key works. This page explains exactly what happens to your data, in plain language.

1. What we collect

No accounts. There is no sign-up, no profile, no email collection.

No analytics. We do not use Google Analytics, Plausible, PostHog, session recording, heatmaps or advertising pixels.

No cookies. The site sets no cookies for tracking or personalisation.

One transient record: while you use the tool, this tab keeps your provider, key and chosen model in sessionStorage so the tester and chatbot pages share it. It never leaves your browser except inside the API request you trigger, and it is deleted when the tab closes.

2. What happens to your API key

Your key is forwarded to the provider you selected (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) so that provider can answer the test request. It is not stored, logged, indexed, shared, sold or used for any other purpose. Full mechanics are on the security page.

3. Server logs

Our serverless functions do not log request bodies, headers or keys. Our hosting platform (Vercel) retains standard infrastructure metadata — such as IP address, timestamp, path and status code — for operational and abuse-prevention purposes, under its own privacy terms.

For abuse prevention we keep a short-lived in-memory counter per IP address (40 checks and 60 chat calls per minute). It holds no key material and is discarded automatically.

4. Third parties

  • Your chosen AI provider receives the key and prompt you submit. Their privacy policy governs that request.
  • Vercel hosts the site and the proxy function.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on the page, which means Google receives your IP address when fonts load.

There are no other third-party scripts.

5. Local models

When you select Ollama or another localhost endpoint, the browser talks directly to your machine. Those requests never touch our servers.

6. Children

The service is intended for developers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

7. Your choices

  • Use a scoped or temporary key when testing, then revoke it at the provider.
  • Close the tab to wipe the stored key immediately.
  • Block Google Fonts in your browser; the site still works with system fonts.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top will change with it. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
