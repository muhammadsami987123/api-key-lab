1. What the service is

The service performs a small number of real requests against the provider you choose, using the key you supply, and reports the result. It also offers a chat interface for the same key. It is a diagnostic utility, not a production gateway.

2. Your keys, your responsibility

  • You must own the key you paste, or have explicit permission to test it.
  • You are responsible for any charges the provider applies to those requests. Tests use minimal tokens, but chat usage is billed by your provider to you.

  • Do not paste keys you are not allowed to transmit to a third-party web application.
